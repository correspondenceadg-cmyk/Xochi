# Privacy Policy

1. **Contacts**: We store the email addresses and phone numbers of your emergency contacts solely to send them alerts if you miss your check-in. We never sell or share this data.
2. **Location**: We store your last known location to include in the alert email/SMS. Locations are permanently deleted 24 hours after a trip closes.
3. **Logs**: Our server logs use truncated trip IDs (first 8 characters) and do not contain your location, email, or personal data.
